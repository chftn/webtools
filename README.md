# 🧰 Toolbox

**Ten small tools for your money and your health — right in your browser.**

No account. No sign-up. No server. No tracking. Everything you type stays on your
own device, and you can export it any time.

**[Open the toolbox →](https://chftn.github.io/webtools/)**

---

## Getting started

1. **Open a tool** from the list below (or from the home page).
2. **Just start typing.** It saves as you go — there is nothing to install and no
   account to create.
3. **Export whenever you like.** Every tool saves its data to a CSV file you can
   open in Excel or Google Sheets, and loads it back later.

That's it. Pick a tool and try it.

## Your data & privacy

- **It stays on your device.** Everything is saved in your browser's local
  storage. Nothing is ever uploaded.
- **Nothing is tracked.** No analytics, no cookies, no accounts.
- **Export before you clear.** Clearing your browser's site data will delete
  everything — so export first if you want to keep it.
- **Take it with you.** Each tool exports a CSV (and imports it back). The home
  page also downloads a **full JSON backup** of all your tools at once.

---

## Tips

- **Bookmark the tool you use most** — it opens instantly, even offline.
- **Export once a month.** A CSV in your own files is a safety net your browser
  can't give you.
- **One browser, one dataset.** Your data lives per-browser, so it won't follow
  you between devices unless you export and import it.
